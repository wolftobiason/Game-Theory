02 · Foundation
Find Your Specific Knowledge

Specific knowledge cannot be trained for. It's found by pursuing your genuine curiosity and passion, not what's hot right now. Answer honestly — this is for you.

Q 01
What could you talk about for hours without getting paid, and people would still seek you out for?
Hint: this isn't "coding" or "finance" — go one level deeper. What specifically?
Q 02
What do you understand better than most people you know — and why do you think that is?
Hint: think about what others ask YOU for help on, even when you haven't promoted yourself as an expert.
Q 03
What is the intersection of skills you have that is unusual — even if no individual skill is unique?
Hint: 1-in-100 × 1-in-100 = 1-in-10,000. What's your stack?
Q 04
If you had 5 years and no financial pressure, what would you build or research?
Hint: ignore what's "practical." This reveals your actual drive.
Q 05
What is something you believe that most people in your field would disagree with?
Hint: contrarian views built on real understanding are the seeds of specific knowledge.
