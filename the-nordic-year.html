Assumptions
  • Ratings are drawn from a normal distribution. City sets the mean and spread. About one in fifteen women you approach comes out at or below 6.5, one in fifteen at or above 9.5.
  • Odds of being single fall from 60 percent at a 6 to 10 percent at a 10, so about 35 percent at an 8.
  • You get the number 80 percent of the time from a single woman, 50 percent from a taken one, calibrated at the 6'0" baseline.
  • Only single women go out with you, and every number from a single woman becomes one date. No flake rate.
  • Height scales the close rate and the penalty grows with caliber, since a 9 filters harder on it than a 6.
  • Relationships are non-exclusive, so they run concurrently rather than in sequence.
  • No learning curve. Your close rate on day one equals your close rate on day 365.
