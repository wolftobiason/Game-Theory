Strategy · Channels · Network Overlap

Every meeting happens
on a channel

Cold approach, the scene, the app, the mutual friend — these aren't just different venues. They're different information environments, defined by one variable: network overlap — how much of what's true about a man her people can find out. Each channel runs on a different fuel, fails a different way, and rewards a different build.

ONE AXIS · FOUR CHANNELS · ONE PORTFOLIO
The Axis

Network Overlap

At zero overlap, reputation is unavailable — it can't hurt and can't help. At full overlap, reputation is the entire mechanism. Everything else about a channel follows from where it sits on this line.

Anonymity cuts both ways. Abroad, nobody who knows her knows you: no gossip can reach her — but no vouch can either, and nothing you claim is verifiable. Her evolved commitment skepticism has nothing to check your claims against, so it runs at maximum, and the transience discount stacks on top. The anonymous channel is verification-free in both directions — that's its freedom and its ceiling.

Cold Approach

Zero overlap
Signal carrier: the in-person read

The anonymous market. Reputation doesn't exist here — a spotless record earns nothing, a stained one costs nothing. Everything rides on what's visible in the first minutes: presence, calibration, the wrap if it comes up, and mutual fast screening. The traveler's channel by default, because geography resets overlap to zero.

Runs onNerve, calibration, volume, a legible wrap on delivery
StrengthUnlimited volume; full control; works anywhere on earth from a standing start
WeaknessSkepticism at maximum — nothing is verifiable; lowest long-term conversion until a tie is built
Failure modeVolume without conversion — meets that never become ties
Pairs withThe option book: its real product isn't dates, it's qualified ties that mature

Apps

Near-zero overlap
Signal carrier: the wrap, made literal

Cold approach with the first filter automated. The profile is the wrap — six photos and a line must do what a friend's sentence does elsewhere, with no voice, no presence, no vouch. The evolutionary mismatch at its purest: strangers evaluating strangers on self-report.

Runs onPhotographic evidence of the wrap — the surfboard, the stage, the summit
StrengthScale and pre-filtering; intent is declared up front
WeaknessEveryone discounts unverifiable self-report; charisma doesn't transmit through photos
Failure modeCommodity comparison — being one of a thousand near-identical profiles
Pairs withStrong wrap assets from the ladder; move to in-person fast, where the read works

The Scene

Overlap under construction
Signal carrier: repeated witnessed behavior

The recurring shared context — the break, the club, the course, the weekly game. The middle of the axis, and the only channel where overlap grows in real time: every week adds witnesses, preselection gets performed in public, and time — the one instrument that defeats a masked first impression — runs automatically.

Runs onFixture status: same place, same day, months deep
StrengthBuilds reputation and options simultaneously; screening runs both ways over weeks
WeaknessSlow; capped by the scene's size; status may stop at its border
Failure modeOne bad in-scene story — the same witnesses that build you can sink you
Pairs withThe ladder's organizer role: the one who convenes becomes the hub

The Referral

Full overlap
Signal carrier: someone else's staked reputation

The mutual-friends channel — the ancestral norm, and the highest-conversion channel that exists. When she asks about him, good things get said. When a friend sets him up, the friend is spending their own credibility — a costly signal no self-presentation can fake — on a match already screened from both sides.

Runs onYears of clean record + advocates who can describe him and know he's looking
StrengthTrust imported, vetting pre-passed, intent legible — highest long-term conversion
WeaknessLowest volume; can't be rushed; needs the network built years earlier
Failure modeOne blown referral — mistreat an introduction and the channel closes for good
Pairs withThe vouch machinery: deposits, the negativity veto, the “I'm looking” sentence

The Portfolio Doctrine

The channels aren't rivals — they're stages of the same pipeline, running on different clocks. Cold approach and apps generate raw contacts instantly, anywhere; the scene converts presence into reputation over months; the referral converts reputation into pre-vetted introductions over years. The mistake is living on one channel: all-cold means permanent maximum skepticism; all-referral means waiting years for volume that may never come. Generate cold, qualify through the read, mature in the book, and let the vouch close what the approach opened.
